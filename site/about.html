The data is small: one branded product, similar indoor scenes, and photos from the same session can land in both splits. The near-perfect mAP@0.5 says more about this narrow setting than about brand detection in the wild. Expect weaker results on very different photos.

How it was built.

Collect and label

123 photos with varied angles, lighting and occlusion, boxed by hand in MATLAB Image Labeler.

YOLOv2 in MATLAB

A ResNet-50 backbone trained for 20 epochs on CPU in about nine minutes. Mean anchor IoU 0.815.

Port to YOLO11

The MATLAB ground truth is decoded in Python without MATLAB, converted to YOLO format, and scored with standard mAP.

Ship to the browser

The trained model is exported to ONNX and runs here with ONNX Runtime Web, with letterboxing and non-max suppression in JavaScript.
